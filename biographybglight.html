___________ ♦ Background ♦

___________________________________________________ The Beginning

Once a child that avoided the world beyond the white gates and the red-painted walls. Wasn't amomg the children whose time were spent down the road, playing game of childrens nor sportsmen. While they were out there, I was in. I was disciplined and determined at studying. I loved weekdays even to the point of being disheartened upon hearing class suspensions the next day. Almost forgot, I studied in Perez Elementary School of Meycauayan City, Bulacan


_____________________________ The Downfall by the Deceived Heart

My adventure sailed smoothly. Top 4 in 1st Grade and then Top 2 in 2nd. My parents had high expectations out of me, they were loving by the way. That is until I was gifted a tablet for my excellent academic performance. I played and played in it, I found it more fun than what I used to do. Truly distracted, not even batting an eye to my homeworks, even to the point of choosing not to attend sunday church with my family. My school performance degraded little by little without me noticing, until the end of my elementary days. All that mattered to me, was that I was happy, nothing more.


___________________________ The Awakening of an Accursed Mind

In spite being under what I considered 'curse', fortunately I was able to pass the Special Science Class Program Exam of Meycauayan National High School, to be a part of them. I am happy, I am proud. Yet during my Grade 7 days, what I've crossed paths with were stress, pressure, and fright. Low scores, countless responsibilities, and failing grades, truly I was helpless. Even my father once had thrown my notebook at me after unveiling that I didn't do my assignments. From these struggles, I have came to my senses of what I've been doing wrong. However, despite falling onto my knees, I didn't think of myself as a lost cause. This challenging chapter made me face hardships that shaped me to become who I am now.


_____________________________ The Redirection and Redemption

Told myself I'll take education seriously and never will I fail again. Then pandemic appeared, Online Class had emerged. This time, everything had shifted online. The pass of soft copies instead of physical ones enabled me to deliberately climb up from the pit I've dug myself into. It was a struggle some times, yet I've managed to receive With Honors award. I've done the same thing the next year and received the same said award.


______________________________________________________ I Have Returned

Pandemic ended upon reaching 10th Grade. Afraid I was for thinking whether can I still retian what I've worked hard. Still unused to the current ways of the time, yet persevered. I gained more friends which I later had group studies with. I also taught them in mathematics for being excelled in that particular subject because of our great teacher. I was happy, yet happier than I was before. I've reached With Honors award once again. The same thing next year after choosing the STEM strand, which made me meet new friends, and individuals to teach. Truly I was grateful for changing myself and for the Lord and my parents who've guided me all this time.


______________ Beyond The Illusioned Borders of Limits

Before 12th Grade, I told my parents that I'll try the impossible; to reach With High Honors in 12th Grade. However, the sections were reshuffled. I was devastated for being separated from the rest of my friends, yet that didn't hinder me from my objective to break my limits. Made friends in my section, taught a few, met many people who saw me as new, and later believed in me. It was a fresh start indeed. I also had funny friends and classmates, even a few people who led me back to our church. Everything was fun truly, one of the greatest years of my high school life. This is as well as one of the hardest because of having strict schedule, for having nighttime dismissals, and terrifyingly tough teachers. In the end of the day, for the first time, I've achieved With High Honors with an average of 95. My countless friends cheered me on, my loving family was undoubtedly proud. My level of happiness exceeds the number stars beyond the midnight sky. Who knew that some failure back in the day, would be able to return to his feet, and spread his wings to soar the high skies?
