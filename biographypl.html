______________________________________________________ New Chapter

It is my first year college in Manila Central University, as well as my first year of attending classes outside of my hometown situated in Meycauayan City, Bulacan. Time management may be difficult due to the tightness of my schedule, but that would'nt hold me back from doing my extent, nor would be able to limit it.


___________________________________________________ The Neverending Love

All throughout my journey, what made it possible was my first teacher; my family. Their unwavering support and encouragement had enabled me to further exceed my potential. With them paying for my tuition and other expenses, I couldn't afford to bring them and everything else they have worked for down the drain. That's why every day, I've chosen to bring out the best in me, in order for me to someday, return everything they have gifted me.


______________________________________________________ A Savior of Mine

I've been afar from him for many years, after being distracted too much by game addiction and social media scrolling. I've forgotten him once, and even became uncomfortable just by hearing his name for the guilt of doing so. Yet even after drifting away from his presence, he still lent me strength for me to stand on both feet. He paved a path for me to come back to his open arms, which brought me assurance for everything. Now that a lost lamb like myself had returned to my shepherd, I shall follow him and his ways, and submit to his plans for me.


___________________________________________________ Band of Brothers

They are among the most important people in my life. We are different people who happens to share the same energy towards one another. Despite the struggle's midst, this bond of ours remains unfazed. An unbudgeable unison in the midst of the storms, as well as in the face of the great academical unrest. Though we may fall once, but we will raise each other, and together we will rise higher than we've been.
