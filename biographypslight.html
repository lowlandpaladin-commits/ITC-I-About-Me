____________________ ♦ Passion ♦

___________________________________________________ A Spark So Sudden

Once a nobody who had uncertainty in the future. Lacked goals, lacked, passion, stagnant and repeating life. That us until I began to make simple pixelarts, and for some reason I enjoyed it. So I made another, and another, until I was skilled enough to draw on paper. The first one may not be that good, but it became the foundation for my betterment. Then later, on digital art, (See Work Samples) with only a phone and an annoying stylus with a tip that often falls. Time passed, and I've gotten used to it. Others found it hard, while I didn't, for I was shaped by it. And here I am, still continuing to better than I was. Although majority of the time, art block hets the best of me.


___________________________________________________ One's Daydream Fantasy

One time, I've played a random adventure game, and before going to bed, a question passed in my own mind out of the blue; 'What if I made my own story, rather than watching or playing from others?' And thus, every night before I sleep, I daydream or should I say, 'night'dream, and imagining the scenes, stories, characters and their interactions with one another. As more games I've played, and as more shows I've watched, the more expanded my story and worldbuilding had become for being inspired and wanted to incorporate several genres in one world.


______________________________________ The World Building Expansion

Imagination itself isn't enough, a sandbox game that enabled me to further world build was Minecraft. Its capabilities to run add-ons (Custom Objects or Entities different from Vanilla ones.) enabled me to make, conceptualize, and simulate each factions I've ever imagined. This is where my cubic modelling skills had been shaped, as well as my familiarity in 'json' text. And this project was initially named "Combined Fantasies", and was later renamed as "Project Legion", (See Project Legion in Project Sections) that was later discontinued due to the frequent crashing of the app I've used to easily create the .json behavior file.


___________________________________________________ The Written Illusions

After the heavy worldbuilding, I've decided to begin the writing the main story. To this point, this is where my vocabulary and writing skills had honed these recent years. The initial writings were more straightforward and boring for descriptive language was underutilized. Losing phones caused me to restart from the very beginning each time, which made my writing advanced each time. It was like a fresh start after a devastating loss. Nowadays with both Google and AI combined, appropriate words are now easily searched and AI grammar checking as well as feedback had gone useful. (See Projects in Project Sections)


___________________________________________________ The Setting of Sails

I know that not everybody likes to read, and among them is me. I loved writing yet reading is not my interest, wierd indeed but truthfully. And I liked playing games, and due to my little foundation for modelling capability, as well as my understanding of .json file, there I aimed higher than I initially I've been; To create my own game or even franchise if possible, and replicate and express the world found inside my head. It has been my goal every since Senior High School. However of course, there's still tiny steps I must climb in order to achieve it.


_______________________________________________________ First Foot Landed

Introduction to Computing had enabled me to take my first step into coding. The countless possibilities, yet bewildering process of coding .html made me appreciate it even more. I remember this kind of process when I still write function text files that use of minecraft's commands. They were both like puzzles with the goal of achieving a goal or intended feature. I loved such kind of work for seeing your written texts work the way you intended them to. With this mere foundation, I know that I'm one step closer to my dreams.
