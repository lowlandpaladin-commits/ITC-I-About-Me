Chapter I: The Beginning Upon An End


Paragraph I: The Deserter

It is the year 18055, an ongoing dreadful battle is about to end, alongside the unjust war and its treacherous actions against humanity. Clouds were once thick, and the sun that cowered behind it had now revealed itself. The continuous graceful fall of numberless tiny snowflakes from above had began to come to an end alongside the fogginess of the land. The snowfall is about to pass. Amidst the ending of a snowstorm, an exhausted young boy is present on the side of a mountain range with a broad sword stained with blood in his trembling hand. The right side of his face was burnt from the fight, and pain is all that he feels at the moment. Luckily enough, his eyes were not affected. His clothes are thick brown with 404040 fur on its holes. Such thick clothing is stained by ashes and blood, alongside the presence of several tears all over. In these torn holes, his red wounds and scratches underneath are present. He looks back to the capital city engulfed in smokes and flames emitted from the fighting. With his small and discolored spyglass from his pocket, he then gazed at the palace one last time. A once majestic structure now at the edge of being turned into ruins, as a result of violence. In his spyglass, he witnessed the flags of his fellow revolutionists being raised, resembling their cause victorious, yet he did not rejoiced. The view seemed left him in deep thoughts, standing there still. He turned his back from the cities and continued venturing deeper into the forests and mountains, never to be seen again.
