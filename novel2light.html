________________________________________________ Paragraph III: The Huge Setback

_____The creatures made for pillaging had arrived, thus the grand battle had commenced. Artillery cannons and guns were fired one after another. Wingcrafts had taken off from their carrier ships and pushed their throttles to their extent. They were accompanied by missiles that were propelled from their launchers. The Vezetas shot back with various kinds of unidentified projectiles which hindered the armament of man. Due to space's lack of direction, a certain suicidally skilled Wing consisting of three hundred men, commenced a perilous attack and had stormed the creatures from their below head on. All of them fired missiles endlessly to cause maximum damage before each of them slumber into peace for eternity. Peacefully knowing they contributed the best they could for their mother to live on forever. Several of them had emptied their weapons and had lived long enough to reload. However, some did not even get the chance to empty theirs and had been shot down early on. A few had crashed to the creatures due to their engine's speed, and some even were casualties to friendly fire due to man's heavy bombardment of battleship artilley batteries. Humanity had eventually took heavy casualties and had later begun to run out of ammunition, yet the creatures remained uncountable. Number of reserves fell short as well from billions to few millions, forcing them to abandon the beacon as well as its sector. Thus all of them retreated in time to their home planet. On the other hand, the suicidal wing had been left behind. Scattered and surrounded, their casualties had pummelled. One by one, one after another, combat cameras had shut down in front of their wing commander's office. Screams of agony is heard from his radio, coming from his fallen personnels that were scorched into ashes, in the midst of darkness. All combat camera monitors had gone black, except for one pilot who managed to fall back and flee from death, codenamed, 'Ironheart'.
