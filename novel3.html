______________________________________ Paragraph I: The Underground Laboratory

_____It is the year 18044 in the Republic of Radle, where tensions rose between countries and war is imminently knocking next door. Doctor Allen Eurie, a famed scientist who graduated from the country's most prestigious science and engineering academy, was tasked by his superiors to develop a devastating weapon as a response to the impending threat. In the midst of a desert, under the wild sands of such a nowhere, their hidden facility had settled and the project was named; Project Eris. There, the doctor and his team of fellow scientists and engineers, had neverendingly spent their hours. Demanding, yet the passionate team continued with dedication. Days turned to weeks, and weeks turned to months. Finally, a prototype was made; A bright cube-shaped cyan core that utilizes magnetism to attract nearby iron and steel, enabling it to form a gigantic steel body of its own. Progress may be slow due to complexity, but the team's perseverance remained steadfast. That is until the arrival of the countless testing failures, as well as the stagnancy of their research after a year. Moreover, the project had gone too expensive for the government to handle, thus was scraped and abandoned. Doctor Eurie's peers left, yet the doctor's fire has not yet died out. Passionately, he continued the project on his own despite its enormous burden and risk. He took the core with him and resumed the research under his secured basement, without any external funding nor even acknowledging the higher-ups.
